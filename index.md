## Welcome

I am professor for Software Engineering at the [Institute of Mobile and Distributed Systems](http://www.imvs.technik.fhnw.ch/) of the [University of Applied Sciences and Arts Northwestern Switzerland](https://www.fhnw.ch/).

I am **teaching** courses at Bachelor and Masters degree in Software Engineering, Software Construction, Software testing and C# and .NET programming within the department of Computer Science.

My **research** is focused around everything that helps to make software development more efficient.

This includes:

- Agile Methodologies
- Tool supported Agile Collaboration
- Automated Software Construction
- Software Metrics and Analysis

I am co-founder of the [Swiss Agile Research Network](http://www.swissagileresearchnetwork.ch/), SARN, and co-author of the bi-annual survey [Swiss Agile Study](http://www.swissagilestudy.ch/).

I am head of the continuous education program [CAS Agile Organisation](https://www.fhnw.ch/de/weiterbildung/technik/cas-agile-organisation) at the FHNW. It's an industry course for project managers, team managers, project leaders and company managers, especially also from non-IT domains, who plan to move to an agile organisation or are already on their way. Participants learn what it means to make your organisation agile. Read the [blogs](http://www.agileorganisation.org/) from participants in earlier courses for more information.

I'm Certified Scrum Master (CSM) and member of the [Scrum Alliance](http://www.scrumalliance.org/).

We also started the **startup company** [aTouch dynamics](https://www.atouch.ch/). We develop software tools for agile collaboration using extra-large multi-touch display technology. Our agile collaboration wall **aWall** eliminates the need for physical card walls and enables distributed agile collaboration and meetings such as daily stand-ups and sprint planning. Want to know more? [Contact me](mailto:martin.kropp@atouch.ch).
