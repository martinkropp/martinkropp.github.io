My research encompasses agile software engineering, collaboration, software development tools, and the wider adoption of agile practices.

## Selected projects

### aWall — Agile Multi-Project Coordination (2020–2021)

Explored and developed tools to help coordinate multiple agile projects across organisations as agile practices scale beyond individual teams.

### Swiss Agile Study

Research into the use and evolution of agile practices in Switzerland, connecting practitioners and researchers.

### Collaborative development and interactive displays

Work on the application of interactive displays and software-based tools to support collaboration and agile teams.
